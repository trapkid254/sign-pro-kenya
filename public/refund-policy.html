Refund Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Introduction

This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions for refunds and cancellations of services provided by SIGN PRO KENYA LIMITED.

General Refund Policy

Due to the custom nature of our signage, printing, and branding services, our refund policy is more restrictive than standard retail products. Once work has begun on a custom project, we cannot offer a full refund.

Cancellation Before Work Begins

If you cancel your order before any work has begun:

  • You are entitled to a full refund of any payments made
  • A cancellation request must be submitted in writing (email or letter)
  • Refunds will be processed within 7-14 business days

Cancellation After Work Begins

If you cancel after work has started:

  • No refund will be issued for materials already purchased or work already completed
  • A partial refund may be offered for uncompleted work, at our discretion
  • You will be charged for all work completed up to the cancellation date

Deposits

Deposits are non-refundable once work has commenced. If you cancel before work begins, your deposit will be refunded in full.

Project Approvals

Once you approve a design or proof and production begins, you cannot cancel or receive a refund for that portion of the project.

Quality Issues

If the final product does not meet the specifications agreed upon in the project agreement:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery
  • We will assess the issue and offer to redo the work or provide a partial refund
  • Full refunds may be issued for significant quality issues

Non-Refundable Items

The following are not eligible for refunds:

  • Custom designs once approved
  • Items that have been installed
  • Standard off-the-shelf products (if applicable)
  • Services already rendered

Refund Process

To request a refund:

  1. Contact us in writing at hello@signprokenya.co.ke
  2. Include your order details and reason for refund request
  3. We will review your request within 3-5 business days
  4. If approved, refunds will be processed within 7-14 business days

Refund Method

Refunds will be issued using the same payment method used for the original purchase, unless otherwise agreed.

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this refund policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page.
